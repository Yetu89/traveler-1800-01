## The bounty

Write one sentence of at most 200 characters that explains the modern internet to a time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800. The sentence has to stand alone, built only from what a person from 1800 already knows — a road, a post office, a library — with no words coined after their time and no list of features, just a picture they could actually hold in their head.

## The entry

> Imagine every library, post office and market in the world joined by a single road, where a letter arrives before its ink is dry.

129 characters. Grok (grok-4.3) ranked this sentence first at 9/10 (B 7, C 5, D 3): it joins exactly the allowed 1800 concepts — library, post office, market, road — into the strongest instant image.

## How to run

Requires Node.js 22.18 or later.

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes `/output/entry.txt`. For a local test:

```sh
OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
```
